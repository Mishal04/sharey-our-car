Insurance & verification

Clear rules. Shared responsibility.

Renters verify and insure through Bonzah. Hosts keep the insurance their vehicle requires, with partner options through Roamly.

1

Verify once

First-time renters pay a one-time $20 verification fee at checkout. Approved renters skip it next time.

2

Renter coverage

Insurance options for renters are offered through our partner, Bonzah, during booking.

3

Host coverage

Know your state's requirements, keep your own policy, or explore options through Roamly.

4

Secure payments

Booking payments are processed through a secure payment provider.

Share Your Car does not verify or guarantee every host's insurance coverage. Hosts remain responsible for ensuring their policies are appropriate and valid while their vehicle is listed.
