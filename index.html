Insurance & responsibility

Clear rules. Shared responsibility.

Hosts are responsible for keeping the insurance their vehicle requires. We make the requirements easy to understand and coverage easy to find.

1

Know the requirements

Our onboarding explains what coverage applies in your state before your car goes live.

2

Keep your own policy

If your current insurance qualifies, you can keep using it under our platform terms.

3

Partner options

Need coverage? Explore options through our insurance partner, Roamly, from your dashboard.

4

Secure payments

Booking payments are processed through a secure payment provider.

Share Your Car does not verify or guarantee every host's insurance coverage. Hosts remain responsible for ensuring their policies are appropriate and valid while their vehicle is listed.
